Ocean Explorer Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Ocean Explorer is an app for iPhone and Android that lets you dive Monterey Canyon, built from real seafloor data. This policy explains what the app does with information about you.

The short version

Ocean Explorer doesn't collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything the app shows is built into it, and it works entirely on your device.

Information the app collects

None. Specifically, Ocean Explorer:

  • Doesn't ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • Doesn't connect to the internet. The Android version doesn't even request internet access.
  • Doesn't use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos.
  • Doesn't save anything about you or how you use it.
  • Has no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and includes no third-party code that does.

Links to other websites

The app's Credits screen links to the websites of the people and organizations whose data and 3D models it uses (gmrt.org and sketchfab.com). Those links open in your web browser, and those websites have their own privacy policies.

Information collected by app stores

Apple's App Store and Google Play may collect information when you download or update the app, such as crash reports you choose to share. That information is governed by Apple's and Google's privacy policies. We don't receive any personal information through it.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date.
