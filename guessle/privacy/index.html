Guessle Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Guessle is a five-letter word game for iPhone and Android, in English, Spanish, French and Haitian Creole. This policy explains what the app does with information about you.

The short version

Guessle doesn't collect or share any personal information. It works entirely on your device, and what it saves stays there.

Information the app collects

None. Specifically, Guessle:

  • Doesn't ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • Doesn't connect to the internet. The Android version doesn't even request internet access.
  • Doesn't use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos.
  • Has no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and includes no third-party code that does.

Information saved on your device

So you can pick up where you left off, Guessle saves your settings, your game in progress, and your statistics (games played, wins and streaks) on your device. They're never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you've turned on your phone's own backups (iCloud or Google), your phone may include this game data in them. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google, and we can't see them.

Sharing your results

When you tap Share, Guessle hands a summary of your game (the puzzle number, your score and a grid of colored squares, but not the word) to your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

Information collected by app stores

Apple's App Store and Google Play may collect information when you download or update the app, such as crash reports you choose to share. That information is governed by Apple's and Google's privacy policies. We don't receive any personal information through it.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date.
